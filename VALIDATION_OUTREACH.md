## How to record feedback

Record date, role, exact workflow, existing tools, last concrete incident, frequency, time/cost consequence, requested change, and attribution permission. Separate their words from our interpretation. A positive reaction to a pitch is weaker evidence than observing a task or obtaining a usable example.

Do not promise a deployed product, adoption outcome, or ongoing maintenance. No response is not confirmation. One interview is not a partnership. Use “collaborated with” only after actual joint work and agreement about the description.
